Established 2018

Founded on Integrity, Community, and Flavor

Roast & Brew was founded by a team of certified Q-graders and culinary enthusiasts who believed that coffee is much more than a daily caffeine vehicle—it is a sophisticated agricultural product that tells the story of the soil, altitude, and people who nurtured it.

Frustrated by industrial coffee giants over-roasting low-grade beans to mask defects, we set out to build an independent neighborhood roastery. We hand-select only Specialty Grade Arabica coffees scoring 85+ points on the global cupping scale.

"We do not engineer coffee; we listen to the bean and unlock its innate potential through thermal discipline and patience."

— Marcus Vance, Head Roaster

Our 3-Stage Artisan Process

Every bag of Roast & Brew coffee is crafted through meticulous culinary precision.

Stage 01

Ethical Farm Sourcing

We travel directly to origin regions in Central America and East Africa, securing direct-trade relationships with family-run farms that practice organic and shade-grown cultivation.

Stage 02

Precision Thermodynamic Roasting

Using state-of-the-art sensory profiling and cast-iron drum conduction, we track temperature curves to the exact tenth of a degree, caramelizing complex sugars without charring.

Stage 03

Cupping & Fresh Packaging

Every single batch undergoes blind cupping evaluation before receiving quality sign-off. Approved roasts are sealed immediately in one-way degassing valve pouches.
